# Phase 1 — Brainstorming & Ideation

## Problem
Many students and ordinary users find legal-document terminology difficult and may not know how to structure a first draft.

## Proposed solution
LegalEase is an AI-assisted application that converts simple user inputs into structured legal-document drafts.

## Innovation
Multilingual generation in English, Tamil and Hindi makes the prototype more accessible.

## Target users
- Students learning about legal technology
- Individuals preparing a first draft for review
- Small organizations needing structured document drafts

## Expected benefit
Reduce the time needed to create a first structured draft while keeping a clear human-review requirement.
